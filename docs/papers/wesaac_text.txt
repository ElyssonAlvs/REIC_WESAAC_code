Abstract. This paper proposes a Multi-Agent System (MAS) architecture inte-
grated with the N8N platform to optimize administrative and managerial pro-
cesses in agile methodologies, specifically Scrum. It addresses challenges like
sprint planning and resource allocation using the Bart technique for require-
ments elicitation and AI-based agents for customizable workflows. The archi-
tecture automates task prioritization and assignment, reducing cognitive load
on Scrum Masters and Product Owners. Preliminary results from three models
(TextClassifier, Sub-agents, Webhook) show improved scalability and adaptabil-
ity. Expected outcomes include enhanced organizational efficiency. Future work
involves empirical validation in real scenarios.
